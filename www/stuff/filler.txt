So, here's something I would like to have if you think it is possible. I will
describe it by telling you about something I did with my own software. I did
this back in the old, old days in FORTH on our TRS-80 computer no less. I had
various UI applications that had forms with fields to fill out, so I created a
background "form-filler" process (i.e., play function):

a script file (or array) that contained data to fill out (appear to type) into
the respective fields,

a "player" function that would advance through the script one field of data at
a time, 

a "typer" that would type the data characters into the field at a speed that
was human readable, and

it would "hit" the enter/submit button (or the CR) to advance to the result.

There may or may not have been a pause(t) script item, or pause key, to allow a
viewer to think before advancing the play function.
